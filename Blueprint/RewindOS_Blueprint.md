# Rewind OS Blueprint
## Blueprint
Rewind OS\
├── Base: Fedora\
├── Desktop: XFCE\
├── Theme: Windows 98 inspired\
├── Apps:\
│   ├── Rewind Welcome\
│   ├── Rewind Settings\
│   ├── Rewind Technician\
│   ├── Kineat Base\
│   └── Neato (might be app or feature)\
├── Commands:\
│   ├── rewind-install\
│   ├── rewind-update\
│   ├── rewind-repair\
│   ├── rewind-check\
│   └── rewind-health\
├── Configs:\
│   ├── Rewind theme settings\
│   ├── Desktop shortcuts\
│   ├── Taskbar layout\
│   ├── Start menu layout\
│   ├── Neato settings\
│   └── User preferences\
├── Services:\
│   ├── Rewind Health Monitor\
│   ├── Rewind Update Checker\
│   ├── Neato Background Service\
│   └── Welcome Tips Service\
└── Workflow:\
│   ├── Shortcuts on desktop\
│   ├── Windows 98 Start menu\
│   ├── Windows 98 taskbar/system tray\
│   └── Windows 98 on-screen widget (optional)\
Design Philosophy:\
├── Beginner Friendly\
├── Repair Before Reinstall\
├── Retro Outside, Modern Inside\
└── Transparency Over Complexity\
## Pillars
1. Retro Experience\
    • Windows 98-inspired desktop\
    • Retro sounds\
    • Retro branding\
    • Retro widgets\
This is what users see\
2. Maintenance & Recovery\
    • Rewind Technician\
    • rewind-health\
    • rewind-check\
    • rewind-repair\
This is what makes the OS useful\
3. Friendly Guidance\
    • Neato\
    • Rewind Welcome\
    • Tutorials\
    • Beginner-friendly commands\
This is what makes the OS approachable.
